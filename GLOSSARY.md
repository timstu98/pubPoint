# PubPoint

Language for finding a pub or bar that works for a group.

## Language

**Saved group**:
A reusable group of registered users who have accepted membership.
_Avoid_: Search participant list

**Membership**:
A user's accepted place in a saved group. A pending invitation is not membership.

**Search**:
A meetup's participants, starting points, transport choices, and venue recommendations, which can be adjusted and searched again.

**Temporary group**:
The user-facing name for the people brought together in an ad hoc search, without first saving a reusable group.
_Avoid_: Guest membership

**Participant**:
A registered user or guest included in a particular search.
_Avoid_: Member (unless referring to saved-group membership)

**Guest**:
A participant represented within a search without a linked PubPoint account.

**Personal defaults**:
A user's saved home address and transport choice for starting future searches.

**Starting point**:
The location a participant travels from for a particular search.

**Venue**:
A pub or bar considered as a meeting destination. The MVP covers London.

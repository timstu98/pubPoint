# Product ideas

Ideas to explore beyond the current [MVP](mvp-prd.md).

## A worldwide group finds its best bar

A possible TikTok trend: people in different countries form a group and use PubPoint to find the best bar for them to meet at right now. Combine flight information with local travel times; the answer might be a bar near Madrid Airport that works surprisingly well for everyone.

The unexpected recommendation and the group's worldwide starting points could make a result people want to post and compare. Explore what “best” means here, flight availability and cost, and whether the travel estimates make a credible meetup possible.
